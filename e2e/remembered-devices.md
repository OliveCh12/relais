# Short check — remembered devices

1. On the Camera phone, open **Monitor → Share this phone's view**. On the receiving phone, choose **Scan a QR code**, scan and connect.
2. Check the video and **Device saved** on both screens. Wait for link-quality measurements: there must be no green bars before the first measurement.
3. Close the preview, then restart both apps. The device list must retain the other phone without showing **Available** until sharing starts again.
4. Restart sharing on the Camera phone. On the Monitor, wait for **Available** and tap the device name without scanning. Check the video.
5. Close sharing: the device must become offline. Rename it through the information button and verify the name after reopening.
6. Forget the device through its native sheet. Check that it disappears, then pair again with a new QR code.
